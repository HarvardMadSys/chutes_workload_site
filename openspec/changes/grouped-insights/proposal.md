# Change: Grouped Insights

## Why

The Insights hold the paper's 48 figures as 47 cards under six tabs (two
cards share the 48th, a legend), each card with its own one-line
insight. Read in a row they feel like a dump: nothing says what a run of
cards proves together, or what a serving system should do about it. The
paper does say so, in the lead-in of each paragraph and in its seven
Findings boxes.

## What Changes

- **Cards sit in groups.** Each topic holds two or three groups, each
  with a heading in the paper's words, a takeaway of one or two plain
  sentences that says what the group's figures show and, where the paper
  draws one, what it means for a serving system, and the cards in the
  paper's order. The six tabs keep the paper's sections, renamed in
  plain words; each topic's takeaway is the paper's Finding for that
  section, or its closing conclusion where it has no Findings box, in
  simple language.
- **The three highlighted models are introduced** where they first
  appear and named by what they stand for: DeepSeek R1 for reasoning
  (long outputs), MiniMax M2.5 for coding and agents (long inputs, short
  outputs), DeepSeek V3.2 for general chat (near the global curve).
- **The two per-user inter-arrival cards are linear**: the bands over
  time on a 0–400 s axis (the 90th percentile clipped where it passes
  400), the boxes across models on their linear axis, so the gap between
  the three models is seen.

## What it moves

- No answer changes. The page gains group headings and takeaway lines;
  the card titles move one heading level down.
- The group copy is new prose; it is the paper's sentences made plain
  and marked placeholder for the authors, as every line the site wrote
  is.
- The snapshots of every topic are rewritten (the headings, the texts).

## Out of scope

- New figures, or figures moved between topics.
- Where the site's data comes from.
